# Contributing

The best way to contribute is to **open an issue** with a real example — paste the text you copied from ChatGPT or another AI assistant and describe what you expected to happen.

Please do not open a pull request without a corresponding issue. We maintain an internal test suite and will handle the implementation once the problem is confirmed.

**When opening an issue, include:**
- The original text (with the math expression that was not converted correctly)
- The result you got
- The result you expected

Thank you!
